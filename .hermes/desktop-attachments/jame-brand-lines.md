# Jame — Brand Lines
*Inspired by: Italian innovation (Rinascimento / Artigianato) × Change (Cambiamento) × Nous Research (open, unbiased, "AI made human").*

## Brand concept
Jame fuses three sources:
- **Nous Research** — open, unbiased, human-rights-advancing AI; "Artificial Intelligence made human"; infrastructure for distributed, transparent training.
- **Italian innovation** — the Renaissance (*Rinascimento*) as the original model of change-through-knowledge; Italian design's restraint, craft (*artigianato*), and human-centered precision.
- **Change (*Cambiamento*)** — Jame's whole job is turning a task into verified, reviewable change.

**Brand idea:** *Rinascimento, made executable.* Open-source AI execution that treats change as craft — human in the loop, inspection-first, beautiful in its restraint.

## Positioning (one-liner)
Nous says "Artificial Intelligence made human." Jame says: **"Change, made open."** — open-source AI execution that turns tasks into reviewable, human-approved change, on your machine.

## Pillars
1. **Cambiamento** — every task is a transformation you can inspect.
2. **Artigianato** — precise, careful execution, not auto-merge noise.
3. **Aperto** — open source, local-first, no lock-in.
4. **Umano** — you stay in the loop; you decide what ships.
